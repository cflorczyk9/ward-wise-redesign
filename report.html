All fifty wards, and what the numbers can honestly say about them.

Every ward in Chicago, ranked against the other forty-nine on the measures Ward Wise Penlight collects. Each ranking comes with what undercuts it, because a place in a list of fifty looks more precise than it usually is.

How to read this

A rank is not a grade

Wards are often bunched so tightly that first and fifteenth describe the same conditions. Where that happens, it says so under the number.

Some measures are modelled

A few are statistical estimates of what a ward like this one usually looks like, not a count of anything inside it. Those are marked.

Ward lines moved in 2023

Boundaries were redrawn, so measures tied to a ward field describe a differently shaped place before that year.

This is not a scorecard for anyone

Rankings reflect decades of conditions, geography, and investment. They are not a measure of any official's work, and nothing here is written as one.
