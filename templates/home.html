How it stays free.

Public data goes stale without someone paid to maintain it. Membership dues pay for that work.

  1. Organizations join

    Planners, community groups and businesses pay yearly dues sized to their budget.

  2. Dues fund upkeep

    Half of every dollar pays people to keep data feeds running and add each year's new records.

  3. The public site stays current

    Every resident gets the same up-to-date numbers, with no login and no charge.

  4. Residents improve it

    Resident nominations become new measures, which makes the data more useful to members.

The membership is called the Ward Wise Guild. How membership works
